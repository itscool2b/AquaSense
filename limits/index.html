What this kit is not

This hardware has not been deployed in a lake. Specs below are copied from manufacturer pages, not from our field notes.

If a number is not on the linked datasheet or wiki, it is not in this repo.

Sensors (manufacturer claims)

  • SEN0169-V2 pH — analog 0–3.0 V; DFRobot industrial probe. Wiki: two-point calibration. wiki
  • DFR0300-H EC — K=10, 10–100 mS/cm, 0–3.2 V. Library DFRobot_EC10. DFRobot: lab-grade probe, not for long-term immersion. Do not leave it in the water for months; rinse and recap between deployments. wiki
  • SEN0237-A DO — galvanic, 0–3.0 V analog. Fill solution is 0.5 mol/L NaOH. Two-point in firmware NVS (zero solution + air-saturated water); uncalibrated POSTs send null, not a fake 0–20 mg/L scale. wiki
  • KIT0021 DS18B20 — waterproof digital temperature. wiki
  • MS5803-14BA — 14 bar absolute pressure, I²C. datasheet

Conversions we implemented (cited)

  • Practical salinity PSS-78 at p = 0 dbar: Fofonoff & Millard, UNESCO technical papers in marine science 44 (1983). Check: 53.088 mS/cm at 25 °C → S = 35.
  • DO saturation: Benson & Krause (1984) as used by USGS TWRI Book 9, Chapter A6. Check: ~9.09 mg/L at 20 °C, S = 0, 1 atm.
  • DO mg/L: linear two-point from SEN0237 millivolts (zero solution, air-sat water) × saturation mg/L. Uncalibrated → JSON null.
  • Depth: (p_mbar − p_atm_mbar) × 0.01 ≈ metres (1 dbar ≈ 1 m of water).
  • Battery volts: LilyGO V1.2+ 100 kΩ / 100 kΩ on GPIO 35, analogReadMilliVolts() × 2 (ReadBattery.ino). V1.1 has no divider.

Hypoxia on the dashboard

The 2 mg/L line is an ecological rule of thumb for hypoxia, not an FDEP laboratory criterion. Cite it that way if you publish a graph.

Solar voltage

LilyGO documents the T-A7670G solar jack as 5–6 V. The Voltaic P110 (Adafruit 5369) peaks near 5.71 V with Voc 6.94 V. That is the closest 10 W panel we found with a buy link; it is not a perfect electrical match. Do not put 12 V on that jack.
