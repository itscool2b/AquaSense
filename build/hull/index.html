Build the hull

24″ square of 4″ Schedule 40 PVC (SECOORA Levin BOB float), Pelican 1120 on the square, 10 W panel on the lid, cage with five sensors, mooring eye. Yellow or white only.

The labeled isometric is the parts count. The dusk photo is the same hull in lighting — if a probe count disagrees, the exploded SVG wins.

What we copy from Levin — and what we don’t

Float geometry is from the SECOORA BOB build manual (Levin): a 24″ square of 4″ Sch40. We do not copy the Pasco pegboard mast, GLX logger, or four-sensor Pasco cage. Deck on this kit is the Pelican 1120.

Cuts (adult + saw, eye protection)

  1. One 10 ft stick of 4″ Sch40 → eight 15″ pieces (Levin). Leftover is scrap.
  2. Four 15″ pieces + four 90° elbows = the outer ring. Dry-fit so it lies flat (stand on opposite corners) before cement.
  3. Levin: do not use instant-grab cement. Slow-set so you can still flatten the square.
  4. Three remaining 15″ pieces get a cap on each end (six caps) — inner floats, strapped inside the square.

Deck and solar

Strap the Pelican 1120 to the inner tubes so the lid opens above water. Solar panel on the closed lid — never a second box sitting on the cells. Glands on the bottom face only. Optional tray: hardware/scad/tray.scad.

Cage — five items, not six

  • Three industrial probes: pH, EC, DO
  • One thin DS18B20
  • One small potted MS5803 breakout (not a long sixth probe)

Keep the cage off the bottom. Mooring eye on one corner of the square. Reflective tape. Paint yellow or white if you paint at all.

EC probe is not for months in the water. DFRobot’s DFR0300-H is a lab-grade conductivity probe — they say do not leave it immersed long-term. Rinse, recap, and store it between deployments. The other four cage items are the industrial pH, galvanic DO, DS18B20, and potted MS5803.
Checkpoint. You can see a hollow PVC ring, not a foam block. Solar is on the Pelican lid. Five sensors in the cage. Hull is yellow/white — not red/green.
