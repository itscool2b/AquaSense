Step 5 of 9

Calibrate on the desk

Do this before the hull. Cups, buffers, and an adult in the room for the DO fill. After flash, serial (115200) waits 12 seconds for cal so the board does not sleep. Or set stay_awake: true in config.json.

NaOH first. The SEN0237-A filling solution is 0.5 mol/L sodium hydroxide. Gloves and goggles. Do not taste, do not leave open around kids. Follow the DFRobot DO wiki for membrane and fill. If you skip this, skip the probe — do not guess a fill.

Stay awake

USB-A to USB-C, serial monitor, newline. Type cal within 12 s of boot, or put "stay_awake": true on the SD card. Commands:

  • sample — print millivolts and converted values
  • doair / dozero / dostatus — dissolved oxygen two-point (NVS)
  • enterph calph exitph — DFRobot pH (EEPROM)
  • enterec calec exitec — DFRobot EC10, 12.88 mS/cm (EEPROM)
  • sleep — one POST, then the 15-minute sleep cycle

pH (SEN0169-V2)

Two-point with 4.01 and 7.01 (DFRobot library). Optional 10.01 check. Rinse with distilled water between cups. The industrial probe is the one in this kit — not SEN0161. Probe in 7.01 → enterph → calph; rinse; 4.01 → calph → exitph.

EC (DFR0300-H, K=10)

Use the 12.88 mS/cm standard that ships with the meter (and/or a fresh bottle). Library is DFRobot_EC10, not DFRobot_EC. enterec → calec → exitec.

Not a long-term immersion probe. DFRobot sells DFR0300-H as lab-grade: do not leave it in the water for months. This kit is a buoy, so plan to rinse, recap, and store the EC probe between deployments. Wiki: DFR0300-H.

DO (SEN0237-A)

Two-point, stored in NVS — not a fake 0–20 mg/L scale. Uncalibrated POSTs send do_mgl: null. Wiki: zero solution, then air-saturated water (pump air about 20 minutes).

  1. Fill and sit the membrane as the wiki says (NaOH PPE above).
  2. Zero solution on the probe → dozero.
  3. Air-saturated water → doair. Firmware scales that millivolt span to Benson–Krause saturation mg/L at the cup temperature.
  4. dostatus should show both millivolt points. sample in air-sat water should read near the printed sat mg/L.

Temperature

DS18B20 is digital. A kitchen thermometer in the same cup is a sanity check, not a calibration.

Checkpoint. pH 7 cup reads near 7, EC standard reads near 12.88 mS/cm, dostatus has both DO points, sample does not print do_mgl=null. You did not skip NaOH PPE.
