Step 8 of 9

Bucket test

Battery + SIM + a bucket of tap water. One real POST to your ingest — not GitHub Pages, not a central AquaSense server.

  1. Your compose stack is reachable at the https:// URL in config.json (VPS or tunnel). Confirm with python3 scripts/simulate-buoy.py pointed at that URL.
  2. SIM seated. Battery in. SD card with config. Solar optional for this test.
  3. Probes in the bucket — not dry. Keep the electronics box out of the water.
  4. Watch serial: AquaSense boot, then a JSON line, then HTTPS POST status 2xx.
  5. Refresh your dashboard. Last-seen should be seconds ago.

If LTE fails, the firmware still appends CSV on the SD card and retries next wake. That is success for logging, not for the website.

Checkpoint. Your self-hosted dashboard shows a row from device_id in config, with temperature near room water and battery voltage in a 3.5–4.2 V neighborhood.
