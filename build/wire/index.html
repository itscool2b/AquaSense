Wire the box

Gravity PH2.0 cables land on the screw-terminal breakout. Cable colors are DFRobot’s: red +, black −, blue analog. Highlight colors in the drawing are which probe. No custom PCB.

  • Wiki pinout for SEN0169-V2: − GND, + VCC 3.3–5.5 V, A analog 0–3.0 V, BNC to the probe. DFRobot
  • Shared 3V3 and GND. Transmitters stay powered (no MOSFET).
  • GPS SKU: GPIO 21/22 are GNSS UART. Do not use them as I2C.
  • Do not jumper GPIO 4 or 5. Remove the microSD card before USB flash (GPIO 2).
  • Optional carrier schematic (same nets): hardware/kicad/. Default is still this screw-terminal box.
  • PG glands through the bottom of the Pelican. Silicone on threads.

GPIO numbers: LilyGO A7670 ESP32 README. Same table in hardware/pinmap.md and firmware/include/pins.h. If a picture disagrees, the table wins.

Checkpoint. Inside the box: pH 32, EC 34, DO 39, temp 18, pressure 16/17. Five cables. No wires on 4 or 5.
